The spec describes the original SOLAR 42 (voices 1–8, from its 28-page manual,
`~/Documents/Synth Models and raw data/Solar42 Instructions.pdf`). The app is the SOLAR 42N, the
owner's ruling: the panel follows the straight-on 42N photo, 1280 × 1280, measured in its own
pixels, and the 42N manual of 4 August decides wherever the two models differ. The 42N renumbers
the voices (classic drones 1, 2, 4, 5; Papa Srapa 3, 6; VCO A and B), adds BP / LP, MOD L / R and a
distortion to the filters, a sub-oscillator, an OCT +3 / LOW switch and a looping ADSR to the
VCOs, a 1-2-3 program switch per effector side and a speed switch to the LFOs. The definition's
docblock lists the differences in full.
